# Synthetic Chest X-Ray Generation for Pneumonia Diagnosis

Master dissertation, *Master in Data Science & AI Strategy*, emlyon business school (2026).
Author: Ana Farré Matemalas. Supervisor: Saeed Varasteh Yazdi.

## Research question

To what extent can synthetic chest X-rays generated by a GAN and by a diffusion model
improve a pneumonia classifier, especially when real positive cases are scarce,
and what does this imply under GDPR and the EU AI Act?

## Overview

Pneumonia is rare in NIH ChestX-ray14 (about 1.3% of images). This project compares two
routes to synthetic data and tests them on a pneumonia vs. No Finding classifier:

| Generator | How it is obtained | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **DC-GAN** (Radford et al., 2016) | Trained from scratch on the real 5K set, one model per class, 128 px, DiffAugment + EMA | 5,000 pneumonia + 2,500 No Finding |
| **RoentGen-v2** (Moroianu et al., 2025) | Pretrained Stable Diffusion 2.1 on MIMIC-CXR, used zero-shot with text prompts, 512 px | 5,000 pneumonia + 2,500 No Finding |

The comparison is between two practical routes, not between two architectures trained on
identical data (RoentGen-v2 carries MIMIC-CXR knowledge and was trained on PA views only).

### Experimental scenarios

| # | Training data |
|---|---|
| 1 | Real 5K (baseline) |
| 2 | Synthetic only, DC-GAN (2,500 P + 2,500 N) |
| 3 | Real 5K + 5,000 DC-GAN pneumonia |
| 4 | Synthetic only, RoentGen-v2 (2,500 P + 2,500 N) |
| 5 | Real 5K + 5,000 RoentGen-v2 pneumonia |
| 6 | Real 10K (contains the 5K set) |

Optional ablations: lower dose of synthetic positives (1,000 / 2,500) and a variant that
also adds 2,500 synthetic negatives.

### Cohort design (Notebook 02)

- Official patient-level train/val vs. test split, with no patient overlap.
- Positives: any pneumonia label (including comorbid). Training negatives: No Finding only.
- At most 3 images per patient and class in the training pool.
- 5K set: 683 positives + 4,317 negatives, matched on view position, gender, age group and
  follow-up group to remove metadata shortcuts. 10K set extends the 5K set.
- Validation and test in two versions: `clean` (pneumonia vs. No Finding) and `full`
  (adds other diseases as hard negatives).

### Evaluation

- Image quality: FID, KID (Inception and TorchXRayVision features), SSIM / MS-SSIM, IS,
  real-vs-synthetic detector, nearest-neighbour memorisation check.
- Classifier: DenseNet-121 (TorchXRayVision), class-balanced sampler, early stopping and
  threshold on the full validation set, several seeds.
- Metrics: AUC-ROC (primary), sensitivity, specificity, F1, per-view (AP/PA) breakdown.
- Confidence intervals: patient-level cluster bootstrap, paired against the baseline.

## How to run

All notebooks are written for Kaggle Notebooks with a GPU.

1. Add the NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset as input.
2. Run `01` and `02`; `02` writes the split CSVs.
3. Add the output of `02` as input to `03a` and `03b`.
4. `03b` needs access to `stanfordmimi/RoentGen-v2` on Hugging Face (gated; requires
   accepting the terms). Save your token as the Kaggle Secret `HF_TOKEN`.
   **Never write the token in a notebook or commit it.**
5. On a Tesla T4, `03a` takes about 5-6 h and `03b` about 10 h with 30-step DPM-Solver++
   (75 steps would be about 25 h). Run `03b` one class per session
   (`RUN_CLASSES = ['pos']`, then `['neg']`).

## Data and licences

- Images are **not** included. ChestX-ray14 (NIH Clinical Center) must be downloaded from
  its official source; follow its terms of use.
- Synthetic images are not published here. Manifests list how each one was generated

- RoentGen-v2 weights are available on Hugging Face under their own licence and terms.
  Its training data (MIMIC-CXR) is credentialed.

## Limitations

Small number of positives (683 in the training pool, 554 in the test sets), single
dataset and modality, RoentGen-v2 trained on PA views only, differing native resolutions
(128 vs. 512 px), and no radiologist review of the synthetic images. Results are
indicative, not clinical evidence.
